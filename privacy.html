Privacy Policy (hti-uploader)

hti-uploader is a private tool used only by the operator of the Hotel & Travel Insider Japan accounts (TikTok: the operator's own account). It uploads videos that the operator created to the operator's own TikTok inbox as drafts.

  • It does not collect, store or share personal data of any other user.
  • The only data handled is the operator's own TikTok access token (stored as an encrypted secret, never in files) and the operator's own videos.
  • No data is sold or shared with third parties.

Contact: via the Hotel & Travel Insider Japan accounts on X (@travelinfo365) or Instagram (travelinfo.365).
